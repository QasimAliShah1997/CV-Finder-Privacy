CV Finder: Privacy Policy Last updated: October 17, 2026 Summary CV Finder does not collect, send, or sell any data. Everything stays on your device. What the extension does You type a role, company, country, and other filters. The extension builds a search link and opens it in a new tab on LinkedIn or Google. That is all. What is stored on your device The extension saves two things in your browser's local storage: The last values you typed in the form. Your last 5 searches, so you can run them again. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with the "Clear" link in the popup, or by removing the extension. What we do not do We do not collect personal data. We do not send data to any server. We do not use analytics, tracking, or ads. We do not read, copy, or change the content of LinkedIn, Google, or any other page. Permissions storage: saves your recent searches and last inputs on your device. contextMenus: adds the right-click search for selected text. Third-party sites When you open a search, you leave the extension and use LinkedIn or Google. Their own privacy policies apply there. CV Finder is not affiliated with LinkedIn or Google.
